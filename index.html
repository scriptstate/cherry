    Privacy

    Nothing leaves your Mac. There's nowhere for it to go.

    Cherry thinks with Apple's on-device model. Your screen, your words and your files never go to a server, because Cherry doesn't have one.

    On deviceNo cloud AI, no API keys, no accounts. Thinking works offline.
    Encrypted phone linkYour iPhone and Mac pair with a code and talk end-to-end encrypted over your own Wi-Fi.
    No trackingNo ads, no analytics. Only what you ask for goes online, like a website, the weather or a lookup, plus a check of your subscription.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    How much does Cherry cost?

    Cherry has a free plan. Cherry Pro adds Research, all five helpers, unlimited skills and more for $5 a month or $20 a year, with a 14-day free trial. Cancel any time; safety features are always free.

    Does anything get sent to the internet?

    Cherry's thinking happens on your Mac with Apple's on-device model, and your iPhone talks to your Mac directly over your own network, encrypted. Only things you ask for go online: opening a website, the weather, a Wikipedia lookup or research. Plus a quick check of your subscription.

    What are Skills?

    Skills teach Cherry new tricks. Each one has a command you type in the chat, like /pomodoro; anything after it goes to the skill, like /jot call the dentist. On your iPhone, say “slash” and its name. Describe a skill in plain words and Cherry builds the steps for you to check, or build it step by step, add ready-made ones, or run your Apple Shortcuts.

    Which Macs does she work on?

    Apple silicon MacBooks (M1 and newer) on macOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on.

    Can she do anything on my Mac?

    Everyday tasks are done directly, so they're fast and reliable. Long, complicated jobs in unfamiliar apps are harder, because she thinks with a small model on your Mac. That's when her helpers step in. She always asks before anything risky, and you can stop her instantly.

    Do I need the iPhone app?

    No. But it's how you talk to Cherry out loud, watch what she's doing from across the room, and stop her from your Lock Screen.
