Questions

Good to know.

How much does Cherry cost?

Cherry has a free plan, and Cherry Pro adds Research and more, for $5 a month or $20 a year, with a 14-day free trial. Cancel any time; safety features are always free.

What are Skills?

Skills teach Cherry new tricks. Each one has a command you type in the chat, like /pomodoro or /goodnight; anything you write after it goes to the skill, like /jot call the dentist. On your iPhone, just say “slash” and its name. Describe a skill in plain words and Cherry builds the steps for you to check, or build it step by step, add ready-made ones from the library, or run your Apple Shortcuts. Three skills of your own are free; Pro makes them unlimited.

Does anything get sent to the internet?

No. Cherry thinks with Apple's on-device model, and your iPhone talks to your Mac directly over your own network, encrypted. Only things you ask for go online, like opening a website, plus a quick check of your subscription.

Which Macs does she work on?

Apple silicon MacBooks (M1 and newer) on macOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on.

Can she do anything on my Mac?

Everyday tasks work great, and many are done directly so they're fast and reliable. Long, complicated jobs in unfamiliar apps are harder, because she thinks with a small model on your Mac. That's when her helpers step in. She always asks before anything risky, and you can stop her instantly.

Do I need the iPhone app?

No. But it's how you talk to Cherry out loud, watch what she's doing from across the room, and stop her from your Lock Screen.
