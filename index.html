    New: Cherry Code

    She builds apps too.Pro

    Cherry Code is a separate app where you describe a small Mac or iPhone app and Cherry makes it: she writes the SwiftUI code, builds it, fixes what the compiler finds, runs the tests and opens it in the iPhone Simulator or on your Mac. You pick the brain: Apple's built-in model, a coding model on your Mac, or Claude for whole apps.

    A real Xcode project

    Open it in Xcode any time. Six starters: blank, counter, to-do, timer, notes and tip calculator.

    Checked at every step

    She builds after every change, and only says done when it builds and the tests pass.

    Runs it for you

    In the iPhone Simulator or on your Mac, with a screenshot she looks at to check her work.

    Pick the brain

    Apple's model for quick changes, a free local model through Ollama that stays private, or Claude with your own API key, which writes, builds, tests and looks at the app until it works.

    Agent, Plan, Ask or Team

    Let her just do it, see her plan first, ask questions about your code, or let the helpers split the work. An effort slider sets how hard she checks.

    Undo and Xcode

    Every change can be undone. Open the project in Xcode, run it there, or click an error to jump to its line.

    Websites, live on localhost

    Ask for a website and she writes the HTML, CSS and JavaScript, serves it on localhost and shows it right in the app. Dev servers like Vite work too.

    A terminal, on your terms

    She can install packages and run commands. Manual asks before everything, Auto asks only for risky things like deleting, and Full Access never asks.

    Any folder of code

    Open a project you already have, in any language, and ask her to explain it, fix it or add to it, like Claude Code or Cursor.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    How do I install Cherry?

    Download Cherry.dmg, open it, and drag Cherry (and Cherry Code, if you like) into Applications. The first time you open Cherry, macOS warns that it can't check it, because Cherry isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Cherry. You only do this once; after that she opens normally and walks you through the permissions she needs.

    How much does Cherry cost?

    Cherry has a free plan. Cherry Pro adds Research, all five helpers, unlimited skills and more for $5 a month or $39 a year, with a 14-day free trial. Cancel any time; safety features are always free.

    Does anything get sent to the internet?

    Not by default. Cherry thinks on your Mac with Apple's on-device model or a local model you download through Ollama, and your iPhone talks to your Mac directly over your own network, encrypted. Only things you ask for go online: opening a website, the weather, a Wikipedia lookup or research. Plus a quick check of your subscription. If you turn on Claude in Settings → Brain, what you ask (and in Cherry Code, the code it needs) goes to Anthropic with your own API key.

    Which brain should I pick?

    Apple Intelligence needs no setup and is private, but it's small. A local model is free and private too, and much better at code: install Ollama, then download a model from Settings → Brain (Qwen2.5 Coder 7B on 8 GB Macs, 14B on 16 GB, Qwen3 Coder 30B on 32 GB or more). Claude is by far the best at building apps; you add your own Anthropic API key and pay Anthropic for what you use.

    What are Skills?

    Skills teach Cherry new tricks. Each one has a command you type in the chat, like /pomodoro; anything after it goes to the skill, like /jot call the dentist. On your iPhone, say “slash” and its name. Describe a skill in plain words and Cherry builds the steps for you to check, or build it step by step, add ready-made ones, or run your Apple Shortcuts.

    What is Cherry Code?

    A separate app, included with Cherry Pro, where Cherry builds Mac and iPhone apps and websites for you, or works on code you already have. For apps she makes a real Xcode project, writes the SwiftUI code, builds it after every change, runs the tests and opens the app in the iPhone Simulator or on your Mac. Websites run on localhost right in the app. She can use the terminal too, asking first as much as you like (Manual, Auto or Full Access). With Apple's built-in model she's best at small app changes; websites, the terminal and bigger work need a local model or Claude. You need Xcode for apps.

    How do I sign in to Pro?

    After you buy, Lemon Squeezy emails you a license key. In Cherry, go to Settings → Cherry Pro → Sign in, and enter the email you bought with and the key. There's no account or password to remember, and one sign-in unlocks Cherry and Cherry Code on that Mac.

    Which Macs does she work on?

    Apple silicon MacBooks (M1 and newer) on macOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on.

    Can she do anything on my Mac?

    Everyday tasks are done directly, so they're fast and reliable. Long, complicated jobs in unfamiliar apps are harder, because she thinks with a small model on your Mac. That's when her helpers step in. She always asks before anything risky, and you can stop her instantly.

    Do I need the iPhone app?

    No. But it's how you talk to Cherry out loud, watch what she's doing from across the room, and stop her from your Lock Screen.
